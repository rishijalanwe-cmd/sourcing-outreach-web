Your API keys

This is a bring-your-own-keys tool — everyone who uses this page pays for their own searches. Your keys are sent straight from your browser to this site's own backend, which forwards them to Exa / Apollo / your chosen LLM for your request only. Nothing is logged, stored, or shared, and there's no database anywhere in this project. Keys live in memory for this tab only — reload the page and you'll paste them again, on purpose.

How this works, and why it needs your own keys

This page is free for anyone to use — but Exa and your chosen LLM provider both charge per request, and this site has no login. If it used one shared key, anyone with the link could run up someone else's bill with no way to stop them. Bring-your-own-keys means the only person who can ever be charged for your search is you.

What happens when you click "Find candidates": your ICP and your keys are sent once, over HTTPS, to this site's own /api/source function. That function calls Exa to search, asks your chosen LLM to score and shortlist against your must-haves, optionally calls Apollo to find a verified email for each shortlisted candidate, then asks the LLM to draft a connection note and follow-up for each one. The results come straight back to this page. Nothing is written to a database — there isn't one.

Nothing here ever sends a LinkedIn connection request, message, or email automatically. Every draft is exactly that — a draft. You copy it and send it yourself, from your own account.

Runs entirely on your own API keys — no login, no database, nothing stored server-side.
